Calls come in at 2am with the furnace dead and a family in a cold house. A
receptionist asking for a name and a number gets neither. The AI asks what
failed, whether there is a gas smell, whether water is involved, and where the
property is — the four things your tech needs before rolling a truck.

If the caller mentions a gas smell or carbon monoxide, the agent tells them to
get out of the house and call 911 first, then continues the intake. That order
is not negotiable and is encoded in the prompt.
