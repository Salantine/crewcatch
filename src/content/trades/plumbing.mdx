A burst pipe at midnight is a five-minute window before the damage compounds.
The intake prioritizes whether water is actively flowing, whether there is
electrical exposure near the water, and the exact address — then flags the call
critical and pushes it to the on-call number rather than the general queue.

If the caller is standing in water near an outlet, the agent asks them to shut
off the breaker before continuing.
