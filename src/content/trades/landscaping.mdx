Landscape work is a consultative sale. Nobody on the phone will quote a price,
and the job is often decided on a site visit weeks later. What the agent can do
is make sure that site visit happens and that you know what you are walking
into: scope, budget range, property footprint, timeline, and whether permits
are already pulled.

An unanswered quote request does not lose you a $180 visit. It loses you a
$8,000 build that was sitting in someone's inbox while they called the next
landscaper.
