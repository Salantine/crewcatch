Pest control is a recurring contract, so the value of a captured lead is the
annual value of that customer, not a single treatment. The agent records
frequency, square footage, the specific areas involved, and how to access the
property — enough to quote without a second call.

Because the contract is annual, even a modest monthly call volume clears the
retainer. Run your own numbers in the calculator rather than taking ours.
