Concrete fails at pour time, not at estimate time. The intake captures
structural dimensions, material preference, and ground conditions — the three
things that decide whether a quote is a straight bid or a change-order
conversation later.

Slab work is materially scheduled, so timeline flexibility is captured up front
to avoid booking a crew into a week the homeowner cannot pour.
